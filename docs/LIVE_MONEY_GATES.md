# Live Money Promotion Gates

Live trading remains disabled until all required gates are independently satisfied.

- Positive out-of-sample expectancy after fees and slippage
- Walk-forward validation across multiple regimes
- Drawdown and stress-test limits satisfied
- Paper trading meets minimum observation/trade thresholds
- Shadow mode matches expected execution behavior
- Duplicate-order, stale-price, disconnect, and kill-switch tests pass
- India broker/API compliance requirements verified for the chosen broker
- US account permissions and India-resident cross-border constraints verified
- Human approval for initial micro-capital activation

A daily profit target is never an obligation to trade.
