# Founder Inputs Required for External Integration

Internal engineering can proceed without these, but external connectivity cannot be completed until these choices are made.

1. Primary market-data provider for India/US/global assets.
2. India broker/API provider for paper and eventually live execution.
3. US broker/API provider for paper and eventually live execution.
4. Production hosting target and region.
5. Whether news/fundamentals data should be paid/licensed at launch or deferred.
6. AI provider preference for qualitative/news intelligence, if enabled.
7. Initial commercial launch jurisdiction(s) and customer type: internal-only, advisory/research SaaS, or execution-enabled service.
8. Initial live-capital ceiling and approval policy when live mode is eventually authorized.

No real-money activation should occur until these are explicitly resolved.
