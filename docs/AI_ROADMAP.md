# AI / Quant Roadmap

1. Data quality and point-in-time market history.
2. Feature store with technical, fundamental, macro, options, sentiment, and cross-asset factors.
3. Baseline deterministic strategies before machine learning.
4. Regime classifiers and calibrated probability models.
5. Ensemble/meta-model that ranks opportunities by expected value, confidence, and downside.
6. Portfolio optimizer with correlation, volatility, liquidity, and concentration constraints.
7. Adaptive stop/target engine using volatility and market structure.
8. News/event reasoning with LLMs, never direct unbounded order authority.
9. Model registry, champion/challenger evaluation, drift detection, and automatic deactivation on degradation.
10. Paper -> shadow -> human approval -> capped autonomous capital promotion path.

No model may bypass the risk firewall, compliance policy, kill switch, or tenant-level capital limits.
