# Product Vision

The platform is designed as a global multi-asset quantitative intelligence and execution operating system, capable of serving individual investors, advisors, proprietary desks, and future SaaS customers.

## Product principles
- Capital preservation before return maximization.
- No forced trades and no guaranteed-return claims.
- Every trade must have measurable expected value and a bounded risk plan.
- Prediction, sizing, compliance, and execution remain independently testable.
- All live-money actions are tenant-scoped, auditable, revocable, and fail closed.

## Asset coverage target
Equities, ETFs, indices, options, futures, FX, commodities, precious/base metals, bonds, funds, and regulated digital assets where jurisdictionally permitted.

## Intelligence stack
Market regime detection, technical/statistical factors, fundamental data, macro data, options volatility/Greeks, news/event NLP, cross-asset relationships, portfolio correlations, anomaly detection, signal calibration, and model-drift monitoring.

## Commercial readiness
Multi-tenancy, broker/data-provider adapters, entitlement-aware features, regional compliance policies, usage metering, audit logs, model versioning, reproducible decisions, and enterprise-grade observability are first-class architecture concerns.
